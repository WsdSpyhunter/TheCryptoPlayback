Market Infrastructure · Decrypt
OKX and ICE File for 24/7 Tokenized Equity Trading
A joint venture between OKX and ICE, the owner of the NYSE, filed with the SEC under its new Innovation Exemption. It would offer 24/7 tokenized trading in more than 60 US-listed stocks, including Nvidia and SpaceX, paired with stablecoins.
Why it matters: It is a formal link between exchange infrastructure and crypto rails. Approval and scope are the items to watch.

Banking · Decrypt
Community Bankers Sue Over OCC Trust Charters for Crypto Firms
The Independent Community Bankers of America filed suit arguing that OCC national trust charters give crypto firms a "side door into the banking system" without the safeguards that apply to banks.
Why it matters: The outcome could shape how easily crypto companies connect to the traditional financial system.
