Miner Health / Network Activity

978 EH/sStable

What It Measures

The Bitcoin network's total hash rate (mining power securing the network, in exahashes/second), its trend into the next difficulty adjustment, and current mempool congestion (pending transactions and required fees).

How It's Calculated

Hash rate and the difficulty-change estimate come directly from mempool.space, computed from actual observed block times since the last retarget - not a projection of our own. A next-retarget change of ±5% or more reads Hash Rate Rising/Falling; anything smaller reads Stable.

Why It Matters

Hash rate is Bitcoin's actual security budget - a rising hash rate means more real-world computing power is committed to securing the network. Mempool congestion and fees are a direct read on how much genuine transaction demand the network is seeing right now.

Data Source & Update Frequency

mempool.space's free public API, no key required. Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes.
