Risk Radar

MODERATE

What It Measures

A simple read on how turbulent current market conditions are — Low, Moderate, or Elevated.

How It's Calculated

A Crypto Playback score built from two real inputs: how extreme the Fear & Greed reading is, and the average size of the 24-hour price move across our 6 tracked coins. Extreme sentiment (Fear & Greed ≥80 or ≤20) adds 2 points, borderline extreme (≥70 or ≤30) adds 1. Average 24h volatility ≥8% adds 2 points, ≥4% adds 1. 0–1 points reads Low, 2–3 reads Moderate, 4+ reads Elevated.

Why It Matters

A temperature check on current conditions, not a forecast of what happens next.

Data Source & Update Frequency

Computed by The Crypto Playback from Alternative.me and CoinGecko data — not pulled from any third-party "risk" API. Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes.
