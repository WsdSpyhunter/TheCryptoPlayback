Top story

A Democratic sweep of Congress reshuffled crypto's regulatory odds just as bitcoin clawed back losses and Wall Street kept building on-chain plumbing regardless of who's in charge.

Here’s A Story You May Have Missed!

A Judge Says a Killer's Bitcoin Wasn't Proven to Be Blood Money

A Nova Scotia judge ordered a Bitcoin wallet returned to the family of William Sandeson, a Canadian convicted of murdering fellow student Taylor Samson in 2015, after prosecutors failed to link the crypto holdings to the crime. It's a strange footnote in a dark case: the conviction stands, but the on-chain assets get to go home. A reminder that tracing a wallet to a wrongdoing is a different legal bar than tracing a person to it.
Read more at Decrypt →

CLARITY Act's Odds Just Cratered

TD Cowen analysts say the Democratic sweep, paired with Sherrod Brown's return to the Senate, could kill the CLARITY Act for two years and make any industry-backed version 'impossible' as Democrats ramp up investigations into Trump's crypto ties.

The industry spent heavily trying to insulate itself from exactly this outcome, and it didn't work. Expect a lot more subpoena headlines and a lot less legislative progress for a while.

Read more at Bitcoin Magazine →

Bitcoin Shakes Off the Midterms, Washington Fight Isn't Over

Bitcoin bounced back after Democrats took control of Congress, but a stalled market structure bill and massive industry campaign spending that didn't buy the outcome crypto wanted show just how exposed the space is to shifting political winds.

Price recovering fast is the easy part. The harder question is whether crypto's political capital recovers at the same speed, and right now that looks less certain.

Read more at Decrypt →

Coinbase Points at Its Cold Storage After Binance's $6.3 Billion Breach

Coinbase says 99% of customer assets sit in cold storage and that it wasn't affected by Binance's $6.3 billion hack.

It's a convenient moment for a competitor to remind everyone of its custody architecture, but that doesn't make the underlying point less real: cold storage ratios are suddenly a very live marketing line again.

Read more at The Block →

Stolen Binance Funds Keep Moving Through THORChain

The attackers behind the Binance hack moved 81% of the stolen Solana and continued routing funds through THORChain after the protocol resumed swaps, with RUNE jumping as the laundering activity picked back up.

Watching a decentralized protocol become the preferred off-ramp for stolen funds is an uncomfortable but familiar pattern, and it raises the same old question about where decentralization ends and complicity begins.

Read more at CoinDesk →

Morgan Stanley Puts Stocks and Treasuries on Chain

Morgan Stanley says it has converted U.S. equities and Treasuries into tokenized form through the newly launched DTCC Tokenization Service, which 20 Wall Street firms have signed up to use.

This is the kind of quiet infrastructure story that matters more long-term than any single price move. When the plumbing of Wall Street itself goes on-chain, it stops being a crypto story and starts being a market-structure story.

Read more at Decrypt →

SEC Staff Gives Canton Coin a Nod as Tokenization Heats Up

SEC staff said they view Canton Coin as a digital commodity and won't recommend enforcement over how it's minted, arriving a week after DTCC launched a tokenization service that runs partly on the Canton Network.

A no-action letter isn't a law, but regulators signaling comfort with a specific tokenization architecture while Wall Street is actively building on it is a meaningful green light, even if it's a cautious one.

Read more at Decrypt →

Circle Cuts Staff Even as Revenue Climbs

The USDC issuer disclosed a 5% staff reduction in its earnings release, booking $9.1 million in restructuring costs as reserve income fell 8% even in a quarter where overall revenue hit $865 million.

Layoffs during a profitable, revenue-growing quarter usually mean a company is bracing for pressure it hasn't shown investors yet. Falling reserve income on a stablecoin business is exactly the kind of pressure worth watching.

Read more at Decrypt →

Block Sets Aside $764 Million as Bitcoin Profit Halves

Block agreed in principle to a $764 million deferred-prosecution deal over anti-money-laundering failures at Cash App, a sum that exceeds its $566 million Q3 profit, while the company's bitcoin gross profit fell 50% after it cut trading fees.

A settlement bigger than the quarter's profit is a rough headline no matter how you frame it, and pairing it with a halved bitcoin business shows how much fee-cutting can cost even a company with Jack Dorsey's backing.

Read more at Bitcoin Magazine →

Ethereum Dev Eyes Quantum Resistance a Year Early

Ethereum researcher Justin Drake said it is their 'hope' that Ethereum will achieve its post-quantum and post-AI transition by December 2028, a year ahead of the network's prior target.

Quantum resistance has long felt like a someday problem for crypto. Moving the target date earlier, even as a stated hope rather than a commitment, suggests the someday is starting to feel more like a somewhat-soon.
