DeFi Pulse

$95.0BShrinking

What It Measures

Total Value Locked (TVL) across DeFi protocols — lending markets, DEXs, staking, and more — combined across every chain DefiLlama tracks.

How It's Calculated

Total TVL today vs. 7 days ago. A positive 7-day change reads Growing, negative reads Shrinking.

How This Differs From Stablecoin Liquidity

Stablecoin Liquidity tracks dry powder sitting in USD-pegged tokens, ready to deploy but not yet used. TVL tracks capital already deployed into DeFi protocols — and moves with the price of what's locked (a broad rally can lift TVL with zero new deposits), not purely a fresh-capital signal.

Why It Matters

A rising TVL means more capital and activity is flowing into DeFi protocols specifically, beyond just holding or trading spot assets.

Data Source & Update Frequency

DefiLlama's free TVL API (api.llama.fi). Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes.
