Capital Flow

37Distribution

Last updated OCT. 9, 2026 · 11:16 AM UTC

What It Measures

Whether price and sector breadth, weighted against sentiment, currently lean toward accumulation or distribution.

How It's Calculated

60% weight on breadth (the share of our 6 tracked coins and tracked sectors that are positive over 24 hours) plus 40% weight on the Fear & Greed value, scaled 0–100. A score of 60+ reads Accumulation, 40–59 reads Mixed, below 40 reads Distribution.

An Honest Note On The Name

This is not built from ETF flows, exchange order flow, or on-chain institutional transaction data — it's a composite of price/sector breadth and sentiment.

Why It Matters

Distinguishes whether a move is broad-based across many assets, or being carried by just a couple of large coins.

Data Source & Update Frequency

Computed by The Crypto Playback from CoinGecko and Alternative.me data. Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes.
