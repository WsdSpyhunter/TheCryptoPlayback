What It Measures

Whichever of the top 10 coins in the crypto market (by market cap, stablecoins excluded) moved the most — up or down — over the last 24 hours.

How It's Calculated

We take the 10 largest coins by market cap (stablecoins excluded), rank them by the absolute size of their 24-hour price change, and surface the single biggest mover, in either direction. The Top 6 Market ticker is a separate, smaller list.

Why It Matters

Highlights where the action is actually concentrated, instead of just reporting that "the market was up."

Data Source & Update Frequency

CoinGecko public markets API. Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes.
